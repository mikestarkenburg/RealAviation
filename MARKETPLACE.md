## Full Description

Four real aviation actions for pilots and aviation enthusiasts:

**ATIS - Single Airport**
Live ATIS data with flight category color coding. Press to cycle through ATIS letter, time, wind, visibility, clouds, temperature, and altimeter. Background color shows VFR (green), MVFR (blue), IFR (red), or LIFR (magenta).

**ATIS - Multiple Airports**
Monitor ATIS letters and flight categories across multiple airports on a single key. Short press cycles airports, long press opens the ATIS page in your browser.

**Flight Tracker**
Track any flight by number or tail number. Works free with OpenSky Network (altitude, speed, heading) or enhanced with a FlightAware AeroAPI key (full status, departure/arrival times, gate info). Short press cycles pages, long press opens FlightAware.

**Airport Delays**
Real-time FAA delay status — ground delay programs, ground stops, and closures. Free with the FAA NAS Status API, or enhanced with AeroAPI for individual flight delay counts. Color coded: green (clear), amber (delays), red (ground stop), dark red (closure).

All actions work out of the box with no API keys required. Optional FlightAware AeroAPI key unlocks enhanced flight tracking and delay detection.
